Privacy Policy (GDPR)

1. Data Controller and Collection

Emendion AB (Org. nr: 559428-3755) is the controller of your personal data. We value your privacy and only collect the personal data necessary to fulfill our contract with you when making a purchase, such as your name, delivery address, email address, and phone number.

2. Purpose and Legal Basis

Your data is processed on the legal basis of fulfilling our purchase agreement and legal obligations. We use your information to deliver your hardware products, handle customer service inquiries, share necessary shipping details with carriers (such as PostNord), process secure payments via Stripe, and comply with statutory bookkeeping requirements (where transaction records are stored for 7 years).

3. Your Rights

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to request access to a copy of the personal data we hold about you, request rectification of inaccurate data, and request erasure of your data, provided it does not conflict with statutory archival requirements under the Bookkeeping Act.
